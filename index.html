01 / THE SYSTEM

A launch is a system of decisions.
Most teams only see the token.

Launch outcomes are shaped before the contract address exists: how crowded the story is, how the first supply is distributed, what the creator can extract, how much liquidity absorbs the first wave and what the public can verify. Mellmon turns those hidden choices into a model that can be inspected before deployment.

01 / INGEST

Describe the launch

Narrative, ticker, source links, allocation intent, liquidity range and fee policy enter one structured brief.

intent → structured inputs
02 / MODEL

Test the pressure

AI agents compare narrative collisions, concentration, curve pressure and incentive failure modes.

inputs → scenario graph
03 / RESOLVE

Improve the structure

Mellmon proposes bounded changes and shows what each change improves or compromises.

scenario → decisions
04 / PUBLISH

Make it legible

A signed Launch Brief records the final terms, known limits and source timestamp before deployment.

decisions → public record

03 / AGENT LAYERS

AGENT 01

Narrative scanner

Compares the proposed token against recent tickers, source posts, domains, repositories and visual fingerprints. It reports collisions with evidence instead of reserving a name or blocking a launch.

  • source timestamp
  • semantic overlap
  • visual similarity
  • duplicate context
AGENT 02

Distribution model

Tests known allocations under multiple participation scenarios and surfaces the wallets that can dominate early price discovery.

AGENT 03

Liquidity stress

Models buy pressure, sell pressure, slippage bands and curve progression without claiming to predict price.

AGENT 04

Incentive critic

Looks for fee routes and creator terms that reward hidden extraction or leave builders with no reason to continue.

AGENT 05

Genesis observer

After deployment, Mellmon compares the public brief with the first observable market activity. Deviations are recorded as context, not converted into automatic accusations.

05 / OPERATING PRINCIPLES

01

AI advises. Wallets authorize.

Mellmon can analyze and assemble decisions. It never needs a seed phrase, and it cannot sign a transaction for the user.

02

Evidence stays attached.

Every warning links back to the source, account, allocation or observed transaction that produced it.

03

Uncertainty stays visible.

Scores express a model's current view. They are not safety labels, price forecasts or guarantees of a fair market.

04

The market remains open.

Mellmon improves information and launch structure without claiming ownership of a ticker or blocking permissionless participation.
